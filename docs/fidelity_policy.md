# LHμC-VirtualLab — Fidelity and validation labelling policy (G0–G5, P0–P5)

**Document date:** 2026-10-28 · **Project phase:** Phase 0 (evidence and feasibility audit) ·
**Owner:** David Hausler · **Status:** binding policy for the two labels every object and every
result in this project must carry.

Binding rules implemented here: `CLAUDE.md` §1.7 ("Fidelity (G0–G5) and validation (P0–P5)
labels are independent and must be shown wherever an object or result is displayed or
exported"), §6 ("Do not claim a digital twin, engineering accuracy or validation that has not been
performed"). Companion documents: `docs/validation_plan.md` §1 (entry criteria of each P-level
and the checks that exist), `docs/architecture.md` §4 and §7 (schemas and the three geometry
representations), `docs/synthetic_data_policy.md` (synthetic inputs cap both labels),
`docs/limitations.md` §8 (statements the platform must not make).

---

## 1. Two labels, two questions

| Label | Question it answers | Enum (`schemas/provenance.schema.json#/$defs/…`) |
|---|---|---|
| **G — geometry fidelity** | *How faithfully does this object's shape, size and placement represent an engineered LHμC component?* | `geometry_fidelity`: G0 … G5 |
| **P — physics validation** | *How well has this number, model or result been checked against something independent of itself?* | `validation_level`: P0 … P5 |

A third vocabulary, `fidelity_tier` (`A_analytical | B_fast_simulation | C_surrogate |
D_high_fidelity | E_independent_validation`), describes the **cost class of the computation** that
produced a run (`schemas/simulation_run.schema.json`, `docs/uncertainty_policy.md` §3). It is not a
quality claim and must not be confused with either label: a `D_high_fidelity` Geant4 run on a G1
placeholder is still G1, and it is P0 until a check has been made.

## 2. Geometry fidelity G0–G5

Schema wording (`geometry_fidelity.description`): "G0 visual placeholder; G1 parameterised
conceptual; G2 physics-simulation geometry; G3 preliminary engineering CAD; G4 validated
engineering; G5 as-built."

| Level | Meaning | What must exist before the label may be used | Typical representation (`docs/architecture.md` §7) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G0** | Visual placeholder | Nothing but an intent to show *where* something would be. Shape and size are illustrative; no dimension may be read off it. Every dimension that is not `null` must still reference a parameter id, and the object is drawn in the placeholder style (§5). | glTF only (B) |
| **G1** | Parameterised conceptual | Every dimension is a registry parameter (`component.dimensions[*].parameter_id`), including synthetic bands; the solid is generated by code in `cad/generators/` from those ids; the component record (`schemas/component.schema.json`) exists with `source_ids` and `assumption_ids`. No engineering detail (supports, cryostat walls, cabling) is claimed. | STEP master (A) + glTF (B) |
| **G2** | Physics-simulation geometry | A G1 (or better) model has been de-featured into simulation solids with a `material_id` per volume (`schemas/material.schema.json`), exported to GDML (`cad/gdml/`, pyg4ometry), and has passed the overlap and volume-consistency checks in `cad/validation/` with the check results stored. G2 is the **ceiling for representation C** and the minimum for any transport run record to reference the geometry. | GDML (C) |
| **G3** | Preliminary engineering CAD | Real engineering input exists for *this* component in *this* machine: a published technical drawing, a design report with tolerances, or an engineering study by the proponents, archived in `references/` with licence and SHA-256; the STEP model reproduces its dimensions and the deviations are listed. | STEP (A) |
| **G4** | Validated engineering | The CAD has been checked against a built prototype or a manufactured analogue (measurements archived), with `manufacturing_readiness` ≥ `demonstrated_analogous`. | STEP (A) |
| **G5** | As-built | Metrology of the installed LHμC component. **Unreachable** for this project; must not appear outside this table. | — |

Rules:

1. A component's label is the **minimum** over its dimensions: one synthetic or unknown dimension
   caps the whole component at G1 (`docs/synthetic_data_policy.md` §6 item 8). A dimension with
   `value: null` (unknown) allows G0 only; the placeholder must say which parameter is missing.
2. Representation B (visual) inherits the label of representation A and "may never display a
   higher one" (`docs/architecture.md` §7). Purely illustrative objects are G0 and are drawn as such.
3. An assembly's label is the minimum over its children; `parent_component_id` propagates it.
4. Placement has its own provenance (`placement.placement_provenance`); a G1 component placed by
   assumption is G1 with the placement status shown, not G0, but the placement assumption is listed.
5. Scenario records also carry `geometry_fidelity`; it is the maximum geometry that *exists* for
   that scenario, not the maximum that is planned.

## 3. Physics validation P0–P5

Schema wording (`validation_level.description`): "P0 unverified; P1 dimensional/analytical
checks; P2 published benchmark reproduced; P3 cross-checked with another code; P4 calibrated on
prototype/beam data; P5 calibrated on LHμC operations." The entry criteria are specified in full
in `docs/validation_plan.md` §1 and are only summarised here.

| Level | Meaning | Summary of entry criterion | Evidence artefact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P0** | Unverified | Default for every new record, model, geometry or result. A number copied from a paper with full provenance is still P0 until a check has been run on it. | — |
| **P1** | Dimensional / analytical checks | Unit string parses (`lhmuc/units.py`) **and** a closed-form relation implemented under `tests/scientific/` reproduces or bounds the value from other registry parameters within a stated tolerance; the check is named in `validation.methods`. | passing pytest, check row |
| **P2** | Published benchmark reproduced | A documented computation (optics, transport, generator, luminosity code) reproduces a published number (`source_id`, page, quotation) within a tolerance fixed *before* the run; immutable run record with software versions, seeds, inputs. | run record + check row + human review |
| **P3** | Cross-checked with another code | Two independent codes agree within a stated tolerance; disagreements written down, never averaged. | two run records + check row |
| **P4** | Calibrated on prototype / beam data | Measured data from a real device of the same kind compared with the model; dataset archived with licence; calibration record under `uncertainty/calibration/`. | dataset + calibration record |
| **P5** | Calibrated on LHμC operations | Requires an operating LHμC. **Unreachable**; must not appear outside this table. | — |

Rules (from `docs/validation_plan.md` §1, restated because they govern labelling):

1. A level is earned per quantity and per scenario; a P1 check of √s does not make the luminosity P1.
2. A synthetic input confers at most P1 on anything derived from it; the assumption id is displayed.
3. A composite result shows the **minimum** of its inputs' levels and its own check.
4. A failing check resets the level to P0 and opens a risk-register entry until resolved.
5. A snippet-only source cannot support any level above P0 for the quantity it reports, because
   the number itself is not verified (`CLAUDE.md` §1.5).
6. *[2026-10-29:]* A `fetched_text` source (connector-delivered text, no page images;
   `docs/internet_research_protocol.md` §5a) can support P1: arithmetic on the delivered digits, in a
   test whose docstring names the access mode. A P2 target taken from it must first be confirmed
   against a page image of the PDF of the same version.

## 4. Independence of the two labels

The labels measure different things and are stored, propagated and displayed separately. Neither
may be inferred from the other, and no UI element, export or report may merge them into a single
"quality" score or star rating.

Legitimate combinations that will occur in this project:

| Example | G | P | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| √s = 5.3 TeV for `LHMUC-DIS2026-1TEV` reproduced by 2√(E_μE_p) in `tests/scientific/test_analytical_checks.py` | n/a (a number, no geometry) | P1 | closed-form check passes |
| Three.js rendering of a 3 km ring tangential to the LHC with correct circumference parameter but invented cross-section | G0 | P0 | illustrative only; nothing checked |
| Parameterised nozzle cone generated from `DET_NOZZLE_LENGTH_MULHC` and a synthetic half-angle | G1 | P0 | every dimension traceable; no physics check; synthetic input caps at G1 |
| GDML of that cone after overlap checks, used in a Geant4 run that reproduces the μLHC "19 %" deposited-energy figure within a pre-stated tolerance | G2 | P2 | the geometry is simulation-grade; the *result* reproduces a published number — the geometry is still conceptual |
| The same Geant4 result cross-checked with FLUKA (if installed) | G2 | P3 | code-vs-code agreement does not improve geometry fidelity |
| LHC proton-beam parameters imported from HL-LHC documentation and compared with published LHC beam measurements | n/a | up to P4 (for the proton beam, not for LHμC) | measured data for a real device |

Corollaries: a beautiful render is not evidence (G0/P0 must look provisional, §5); a verified
number has no geometry and needs no G-label; a P2 or P3 run on a G1 model validates the *model*,
never the LHμC design; and no object in this repository can reach G3/G4 or P4/P5 by work inside the
repository alone — those levels need external engineering input, measured data or a built machine.

## 5. How the labels are stored

| Record type | Schema | G field | P field | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Parameter record | `provenance.schema.json#/$defs/parameter` | — (numbers have no geometry) | `validation.level` with `validation.methods` (required), `check_ids`, `notes` | 796 records on 2026-10-28: 267 at P1, 529 at P0 (counted with `lhmuc.registry.load_all`); none higher |
| Machine scenario | `machine.schema.json` | `geometry_fidelity` (required) | `validation_level` (required) | 13 scenarios: all G0; 11 at P1, 2 at P0 (counted with `lhmuc.registry.load_all`, 2026-10-28) |
| Component | `component.schema.json` | `geometry_fidelity` (required) | `physics_validation` (required) | plus `geometry_version`, `manufacturing_readiness`; no component exists yet |
| Simulation run | `simulation_run.schema.json` | inherited from `geometry_version` → component | `validation_level` (required) plus `fidelity_tier` and `is_interface_demo_only` | `results/` is empty |
| Optimization study | `optimization.schema.json` | inherited from the model's components | via `high_fidelity_verification_status` and `conclusions_blocked_by` | none exists |
| Proposal / experiment | `proposal.schema.json`, `experiment.schema.json` | — | `review_state` / `verification_status` (LLM-extracted content stays `extracted`/`proposed` until checked) | not P-labels, but subject to the same display rule |

Rules for storage:

1. The fields are **required** in every schema that has them; a loader that encounters a missing
   label rejects the record (never defaults silently to anything but P0/G0 at authoring time).
2. A label is changed only together with the artefact that justifies it (test, run record,
   archived drawing, calibration record), in the same commit, with the artefact named in
   `validation.methods`, `validation.check_ids` or the component's `linked_simulation_runs`.
3. Labels are never stored on a module, directory or milestone; "the IR is at G1" is not a valid
   statement — each component in the IR has its own label.
4. The generated `registries/parameter_registry.csv` (produced by `tools/build_registries.py`;
   not yet generated on 2026-10-28) carries a `validation_level` column, so the distribution of
   labels can be audited in one place.

## 6. Display and export rules

Applies to every surface listed in `docs/synthetic_data_policy.md` §5 (UI, plots, exports, run
metadata, reports, conversation).

1. **Always both, always adjacent.** Wherever an object or result is shown, both labels appear
   next to it in the form `G1 · P0` (or `P1` alone for pure numbers), with a tooltip or footnote
   giving the one-line schema meaning. Hiding a label behind a click is not permitted; the
   explanation may be, the label may not.
2. **Provisional look for low levels.** G0 objects are drawn in the placeholder style (wireframe
   or hatched, desaturated, no photorealistic material); G1 objects use a flat "conceptual"
   material; only G2+ may use realistic materials. P0 results are annotated "unverified" in those
   words (`docs/validation_plan.md` §6). Colour is never the only carrier — a text badge or icon
   is always present.
3. **Minimum rule in composites.** A view, plot or table showing several objects shows the
   *minimum* G and the minimum P of its content in its header, besides the per-object labels.
4. **Exports carry the labels per object.** glTF node metadata, STEP file descriptions, GDML
   auxiliary tags, CSV/JSON columns, run records and report tables all include both labels and,
   for P ≥ 1, the check ids or run ids that justify them. A plain image export gets the labels in
   the caption or a sidecar `.provenance.json`.
5. **Never upgraded by the viewer.** The front end displays stored labels; it has no code path
   that computes or raises them. A user-modified value shows class "user-modified" with G/P reset
   to G0/P0 for everything that depends on it.
6. **Reports.** Every result table in `docs/reports/` has G and P columns; a result at P0 is
   described as unverified; a result that depends on a synthetic input shows the assumption id and
   is capped at P1.
7. **Conversation.** When an AI session reports a number or geometry, it states the label in text
   ("P1, reproduced by …", "G0 placeholder, dimensions not sourced").

## 7. Promotion criteria

Promotion is an explicit, reviewed act recorded in the same commit as its justification. Demotion
is automatic on failure or on a dependency change (`stale: true` in dependents).

### 7.1 Geometry

| Step | Required artefacts | Blocking conditions |
|---|---|---|
| G0 → G1 | component record with every dimension bound to a parameter id; generator code in `cad/generators/`; STEP produced from it; `source_ids`/`assumption_ids` filled | any dimension `unknown` (null); any dimension not in the registry |
| G1 → G2 | simulation solids with `material_id` per volume; GDML in `cad/gdml/`; overlap/volume checks in `cad/validation/` passed and stored | any synthetic dimension still present **does not block G2** (simulation geometry may be conceptual) but the GDML metadata must list the assumption ids, and any run on it stays ≤ P1 |
| G2 → G3 | archived engineering source for this component of this machine (drawing/design report with tolerances), licence and hash in `research/archived_metadata/`; deviation list | no such source exists for any LHμC component on 2026-10-28; μLHC, LHeC or IMCC drawings give at most G3 for *their* machine and remain `imported_related`/`analogous` |
| G3 → G4 | prototype or analogue measurements archived; `manufacturing_readiness ≥ demonstrated_analogous` | outside the project's means |
| G4 → G5 | installed-component metrology | requires a built LHμC |

### 7.2 Validation

| Step | Required artefacts | Blocking conditions |
|---|---|---|
| P0 → P1 | unit parse + closed-form check under `tests/scientific/` with stated tolerance; `validation.methods` names the test; check row in `registries/validation_registry.csv` (authored; to be created) | inputs not in the registry; tolerance not justified |
| P1 → P2 | pre-registered tolerance; published target with `source_id`, page, quotation; immutable run record; human review noted | target number seen only as snippet; required software not installed (Geant4, ROOT, LHAPDF, FLUKA are **not** installed on 2026-10-28; MadGraph reachable via launchpad/GitHub; Xsuite, cpymad, pythia8mc, pyhf, pyg4ometry available from PyPI); *2026-10-29:* target taken only from a `fetched_text` source and not yet confirmed against a page image (§3 rule 6) |
| P2 → P3 | second independent code; two run records; agreement within stated tolerance; disagreement analysis | only one code available for the quantity |
| P3 → P4 | archived measured dataset with licence; calibration record under `uncertainty/calibration/` | no dataset of the same kind of device |
| P4 → P5 | LHμC operations data | requires an operating LHμC |

General: a promotion of one object never promotes its neighbours; promotion of a result never
promotes the geometry it ran on; a synthetic input blocks P > 1 and G > 1 for dependents until the
assumption record is retired (`docs/synthetic_data_policy.md` §3, retirement).

## 8. Phase 0 statement (2026-10-28)

- **Everything in this repository is at most G1 and at most P1.** In fact no object has reached
  G1: all 13 scenarios carry `geometry_fidelity: G0`, no component record, STEP, glTF or GDML file
  exists (`cad/README.md`, `docs/architecture.md` §7), and `results/` holds no run record.
- The P1 labels in `parameters/**` (267 of 796 records) are backed only by the closed-form checks
  in `lhmuc/analytical.py` and `tests/scientific/test_analytical_checks.py` (√s, γ and τ_lab,
  naive luminosity with H = 1, Bρ and bending field, σ from ε_n and β*, booster survival model,
  x_min; see `docs/validation_plan.md` §2). They say that published numbers are *mutually
  consistent*, not that the LHμC will achieve them (`docs/limitations.md` §8).
- No P2 benchmark has been attempted; the planned ones (AloHEP luminosity, MAD-X vs Xsuite,
  Geant4 vs BDSIM, generator cross sections) are listed as *planned* in `docs/validation_plan.md`
  §3 and depend on software not yet installed or adopted.
- No synthetic value exists yet (`parameters/assumptions/` is empty); when the first one is
  introduced under `docs/synthetic_data_policy.md`, every dependent is capped at G1/P1 by rule.
- The authored `registries/validation_registry.csv` and the generated `registries/*.csv` were
  not present when this document was written; until they exist the P1 labels are traceable only
  through `validation.methods` strings in the YAML records and the test names they cite.
- Consequently, nothing produced so far may be called a digital twin, an engineering model, a
  validated model or a design result (`CLAUDE.md` §6). The correct wording is "G0 placeholder" and
  "P1 consistency check of published numbers".
